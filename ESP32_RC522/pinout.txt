SDA (SS)     GPIO 5                  SPI Chip Select
SCK          GPIO 18                 SPI Clock

MISO         GPIO 19                 SPI Master In Slave Out

GND          GND                     Ground

The RC522 is connected to the ESP32-WROOM. The ESP32-S3-CAM has a separate
camera-only firmware and publishes frames to the backend over MQTT. Socket.IO
is used only between the backend and frontend.

PlatformIO WROOM project:
- Open ESP32_WROOM_RFID.
- Configure Wi-Fi credentials in include/device_config.h.
- The firmware publishes JSON scan events to esp32/rfid/data and keeps scans
  in an NVS queue while Wi-Fi or MQTT is unavailable.
- The firmware publishes with MQTT QoS 1 and removes a queued scan after PUBACK.
  PUBACK confirms broker receipt, not backend processing.
