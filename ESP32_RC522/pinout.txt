RC522 Pin    ESP32-S3 GPIO Pin    Function
SDA (SS)     GPIO 10              SPI Chip Select
SCK          GPIO 11              SPI Clock
MOSI         GPIO 12              SPI Master Out Slave In
MISO         GPIO 13              SPI Master In Slave Out
RST          GPIO 14              Reset
GND          GND                  Ground
3.3V         3.3V                 Power Supply (3.3V only - DO NOT connect to 5V)

Note for ESP32-S3:
- Pins 10, 11, 12, 13 match the consecutive physical pin order of RC522 (SDA, SCK, MOSI, MISO) for easy straight ribbon cable wiring.
- GPIO 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 form a continuous block of 5 adjacent pins on header J1 (Pins 16 through 20).
- RST (GPIO 14) is located right after GPIO 13 on header J1 (Pin 20).
- Avoid using GPIO 19 and 20 for SPI on ESP32-S3 as they are dedicated to Native USB (D- / D+).
